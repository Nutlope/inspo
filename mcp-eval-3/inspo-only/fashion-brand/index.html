Field notes  ·  N° 04 — i

Eighteen months
at one mill.

The cloth for Edition 04 began on a single set of looms in Guimarães in the winter of 2024. We slowed the weave by a third, washed it twice in soft water, and let it rest for six weeks before it was cut. The result is a linen that already feels like it has been somewhere.

Section 03  ·  The atelier

A small house, three rooms, one rule — make less, make it last.

A  ·  The cloth

Slow-loomed in Northern Portugal

Every metre of Edition 04 is woven at a single family mill outside Guimarães. We buy the whole production run; nothing is left for the open market.

Mill · Tear de Linho Since 2021
B  ·  The cut

Drafted by hand, sized to a body

Our patterns are drawn at one-to-one on brown paper in Bermondsey. Each garment is graded across nine sizes and three lengths, then re-fitted on real people for one full season before it ships.

C  ·  The care

Repaired forever, never replaced

Every piece arrives with a numbered repair ticket. Mend a seam, replace a button, re-line a coat — bring it back to us and we will look after it for as long as you wear it.
