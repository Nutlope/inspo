Goals and pocket money

Saving is easier when the money has somewhere to go.

Every pound in Tenner lives in a goal, a jar or the spend balance. Nothing sits around waiting to be spent by accident.

What parents control

You set the rails. They learn to drive.

The parent app is not a spying tool, it is a settings panel. Everything below can be changed in a tap and takes effect straight away.

Spending limits

A daily cap, a single-payment cap and an optional weekly budget. Defaults are £250 a day and £100 a payment, and you can go lower.

Freeze and unfreeze

Card left in a locker? Freeze it from your phone. Unfreeze when it turns up. The teen sees the same switch, and is told who flipped it.

Where it works

Online payments, cash machines and spending abroad are each a switch. Gambling, alcohol and tobacco merchants are blocked for everyone and cannot be turned on.

Alerts and a weekly summary

A notification for every payment, or just the big ones. Sunday evening you get a one-screen summary of what was spent and saved.

Approve requests

Teens can ask for a top up, a higher limit for a day, or a new goal. You approve or decline in the app, with a note if you want.

How to open an account

Ten minutes on the sofa, card in the post by Thursday.

A parent or legal guardian opens the account. The teen gets their own login and their own app the moment it is approved.

Parent 1

Download Tenner Parent

Confirm your identity with a photo of your passport or driving licence and a short video selfie. This is a legal requirement and takes about three minutes.

Parent 2

Add your teen

Their name, date of birth and the address the card should go to. You can add up to four teens to one parent account.

Teen 3

They set up their app

A link goes to their phone. They pick a pin, a card colour, and set their first goal before the card even arrives.

Both 4

Top up and tap

The card arrives in about three working days. Activate it in the teen app, and your first top up is on it in seconds.

Safety facts

The boring bits, said plainly.

Money for a 14 year old should be at least as safe as money for a 40 year old. Here is how it is kept that way.

Your money is safeguarded

Tenner is not a bank. Balances are held by our regulated e-money partner in a safeguarded account at a high street bank, kept separate from any company money.

Blocked categories, no exceptions

Gambling, alcohol, tobacco and adult merchants are blocked at the card network level. Neither app has a switch for them.

Card numbers stay hidden

The full 16 digits only show in the teen app after face unlock or a pin, and the parent can turn online payments off entirely.

Freeze in seconds, refund in days

Lost cards are frozen instantly from either app. Payments the teen did not make are refunded under the card scheme's rules, usually within five working days.

No data sold, no ads

We do not sell spending data or show ads in either app. The only marketing a teen ever sees from us is a birthday message.

Nothing to borrow

There is no overdraft, no credit and no buy now pay later. If the balance is £4.20, the card can spend £4.20.

Questions parents ask

Can my teen move money out without me knowing?+

No. Money can only leave the card by paying at a shop, withdrawing cash where you have allowed it, or sending it back to the parent account. Every one of those shows up in your app at the moment it happens.

What happens when they turn 18?+

The account keeps working for 90 days after their birthday with the same limits. In that window they can move the balance to any British bank account in their name. After that the card closes and any remaining balance goes back to the parent account.

Does using Tenner affect their credit score?+

No. Tenner is a prepaid debit card, not credit. Nothing is reported to credit reference agencies, positively or negatively.

Can both parents have access?+

Yes. One parent opens the account and can invite a second adult, who gets the same controls. Either can be removed by the account holder at any time.
