Fares

One fare for every crossing, whichever island.

No zones and no peak pricing. A single is a single, from any berth to any other. Tickets are sold on board, in the app, and at the Sølvøy harbour office. Card only on board.

Island residents Registered residents of the four outer islands travel free on foot and pay half fare for a vehicle. Bring your island card to the berth.

The fleet

Three electric ferries, charged between crossings.

Each vessel plugs into shore power at Sølvøy berth A and takes on enough charge for the next round trip while passengers board. Nothing idles, nothing burns.

Weather and cancellations

We sail in most weather. Here is what stops us, and what happens then.

Conditions come from the Sølvøy harbour mast and the Utvær buoy, read every ten minutes. The captain makes the final call, always before the timetable says the ferry should leave.

Cancellation policy valid from 1 January 2026

  • 01
    If we cancel, you get everything back.Full refund to the original card within five days, or a free rebooking on any later sailing. No forms.
  • 02
    Change your sailing free up to two hours before.Singles and vehicle tickets can be moved in the app. Inside two hours the ticket stays valid for the next two sailings on the same route.
  • 03
    Weather disruptions are announced, not discovered.We text every ticket holder and update this board at least 45 minutes before a paused departure.
  • 04
    Stranded overnight, we cover the bed.If the last sailing home is cancelled, Havlinje pays for a room at the island guesthouse on presentation of your ticket.
  • 05
    Passes and ten-trip cards are extended.Any day on which a route is suspended for more than four hours is added to the end of your island pass.
