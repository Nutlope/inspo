Mission

We believe intelligence is not magic. It is structure - learnable, measurable, and ultimately understandable.

Axiom exists to close the gap between what we can build and what we can explain. Every system we ship carries the proof of its own reasoning, and every paper we publish moves us closer to a science of mind that holds up to scrutiny.

The work is slow, hard, and honest. That is the only way it lasts.

E
Elena Vasquez Co-founder & Chief Scientist

Selected results

Recent work, in the open.

01

Faithful Reasoning in Large Language Models

A formal framework for verifiable chain-of-thought, reducing logical errors on multi-step problems by 73% while preserving calibration across uncertain steps.

Chen, M., Vasquez, E., Okafor, A., et al.

NeurIPS 2024

December 2024

 02

Causal Probing: Interpreting What Models Actually Know

A new class of intervention-based probes that distinguish memorized correlations from genuine causal understanding, validated across14 model families.

Okafor, A., Lindqvist, J., Vasquez, E.

ICLR 2024

May 2024

 03

Scaling Laws Beyond Compute: Data Quality as a First-Class Variable

An empirical study showing that data curation quality follows its own power law, independent of model size - with implications for every frontier training run.
