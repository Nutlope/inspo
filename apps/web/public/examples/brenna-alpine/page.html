The house

Larch from the north slope, snow from the sky.

Brenna was a hay barn until 2019. When we rebuilt it, we cut the larch from our own stand above the tree line, stacked it for four winters and let it silver where it faces the weather. Inside it stays honey coloured, and it smells faintly of resin when the stove is lit.

There is no lobby, no bar and no spa menu. There is a stube with a long table, a sauna that opens onto the snow, and eleven rooms that were designed from the bed outward.

Built
1912 as a barn, rebuilt 2019 to 2021 by Studio Prader, Bolzano
Timber
Larch, 212 trees, felled on the north slope, air dried four years
Heat
Wood pellet boiler and three tiled stoves, fed from our own forest
Owners
Lena and Tobias Rier, who live in the small house behind the barn

Getting here

The last six kilometres are the slow part.

The road to Valtura is cleared every morning and closed to cars above the village in winter. Leave yours in our covered garage at the valley station and we bring you up in the house car.

Questions

What people ask before they book.

  • Is there a minimum stay?

    Two nights in every season except the festive weeks, when it is five. We are happy to hold a single night if one appears between two bookings; write to us.

  • Do the rates include breakfast?

    Yes. Breakfast, the afternoon bread and soup, the sauna and the shuttle to the cable car are in the room rate. Dinner is €78 per person for four courses and is booked by noon the same day.

  • Can we bring children or a dog?

    Children of any age are welcome; a cot is free and a third bed in the Loft or Ridge House is €60 a night. Dogs stay in the Ridge House only, at €25 a night, and are welcome in the stube but not in the sauna.

  • What is the cancellation policy?

    Free until 21 days before arrival. Inside 21 days we charge the first night; inside 7 days, half the stay. If the road is closed by snow on your arrival day, we refund everything.

  • Is there parking and ski storage?

    Nine covered spaces at the valley station, €12 a night, and a heated drying room at the front door with a boot warmer for every room.
