Good questions

The stuff you're probably wondering.

Is my banking data actually safe?
Yes. Tally connects through a regulated open-banking provider using read-only access and bank-grade 256-bit encryption. We can see transactions to help you budget, but Tally can never move, spend, or withdraw your money. You can disconnect any account in one tap.
How is Tally free? What's the catch?
No catch. The free plan is genuinely free, and Tally Plus (£4/month) is how we keep the lights on. We never run ads and we never sell your data — being on your side is the entire business model.
Will it work with my bank?
Almost certainly. Tally connects with over 12,000 banks and credit unions, plus most major credit cards. If yours isn't supported yet, you can add transactions manually and we'll notify you the moment it's live.
Do I have to budget every single category?
Not at all. Most people start with just two or three friendly limits — say groceries, eating out, and fun money — and let Tally watch the rest. You can get as detailed as you like, or keep it gloriously simple.
Can my partner and I share a budget?
Yep — shared budgets come with Tally Plus. Each person keeps their own private accounts, and you pick exactly which pots to pool. Splitting rent and groceries finally stops being a spreadsheet argument.
