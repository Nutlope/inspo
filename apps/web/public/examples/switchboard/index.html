§ 03 - Switches & Feel

Three options

The switch is the argument. Everything else is the case it makes.

A keyboard is a stack of springs and contacts, and the spring you choose decides how the whole thing reads. We offer three hand-tuned switches - linear, tactile, and clicky - each measured and lubed in-house before it ships.

Copper Linear

Linear · Silent · Factory-lubed

No tactile bump. A smooth, unbroken press from top to bottom. The quietest switch we offer - built for open offices and long writing sessions where you'd rather not announce every keystroke.
