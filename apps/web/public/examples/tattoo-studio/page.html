The studio

We opened on Stokes Croft in 2019 with two chairs and a wall of flash. We have four chairs now. The wall is still the point.

Every artist here works in black ink. That is not a limitation we tolerate, it is the reason the studio exists: black heals cleaner, ages slower and reads from across a room. The flash sheet is drawn in-house, redrawn every season, and priced on the wall so nobody has to ask.

Custom work happens too, by consultation, with the artist whose hand suits the idea. If you are not sure who that is, tell us the idea and we will tell you the name.

Booking and deposits

You book the artist, not the studio. Here is how it goes.

  1. 01

    Pick an artist

    Flash goes to the hand that drew it. For custom work, choose from the roster or describe the idea and we will route it to the right chair.

  2. 02

    Send the form

    Design or idea, placement, rough size. The artist replies within three working days with a date and a quote. Custom pieces get a short consultation first, in person or on the phone.

  3. 03

    Pay the deposit

    A bank link arrives with the date. The deposit holds the slot and comes off the final price on the day. Reschedule once for free with 48 hours notice; less than that, or a no-show, and it is forfeited.

  4. 04

    Come in, sit down

    Eat first, sleep the night before, bring photo identification. Card or cash for the balance. We do not tattoo anyone under 18 or anyone who has been drinking.

Aftercare

Black ink heals well if you leave it alone. This is the whole method.

The first night
We wrap the tattoo in a clear second-skin film. Leave it on for three to five days unless it fills with fluid or lifts at the edge. Ink and plasma under the film are normal and look worse than they are. Do not re-wrap with cling film.
Taking the film off
In a warm shower, peel it slowly in the direction of hair growth. Wash with lukewarm water and an unscented soap, using your hand only. Pat dry with clean kitchen roll, not a towel, and let it air for twenty minutes.
Days five to fourteen
Wash twice a day. Apply a rice-grain amount of unscented moisturiser after each wash, rubbed in until there is no shine. Peeling and itching start around day five; do not pick, scratch or soak. Loose cotton over the tattoo, nothing tight.
Weeks two to four
The surface heals first and looks milky for a while as the deeper skin settles. Keep moisturising once a day. No swimming, saunas, baths or direct sun until the skin is fully flat and matte, usually four weeks. After that, sunscreen for life keeps black black.
If something looks wrong
Redness spreading past the tattoo, heat, yellow discharge or a fever are not normal healing. Message your artist the same day with a photo; we will tell you whether to see a doctor. Free touch-ups within three months for anything that fell out through no fault of yours.
