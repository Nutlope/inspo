A seed bank is a farm, not a freezer.

Store

Dried to 6%, then frozen

Seed goes into laminate foil at minus twenty. At that moisture and temperature most cereals stay viable for a century, and most brassicas do not, which is why the next two steps exist.

Grow

Six hundred lines a year, in soil

Every accession is grown out and re-harvested on a rotation. Isolation cages for the outbreeders, and a volunteer network of 140 growers doing the rest on allotments.

Give

Free, and yours to keep

Any grower can request up to five lines a season at no charge. There is no material transfer agreement, no royalty, and no clause about what you may breed from it.

Requests open in January and close when the packets run out.

Tell us what you grow and roughly where. We prioritise growers within the climate a line came from, then anyone willing to send harvest notes back at the end of the season.
