The Ceremony

Three movements, four minutes.

Every bowl follows the same quiet choreography - the same one we learned from a tea master in Uji, trimmed to a city afternoon.

Warm the bowl

Boiled water is poured into the chawan and tipped out. The bowl holds a gentle heat that wakes the powder without scorching it.

~ 30 seconds

Sift & whisk

A heaping of matcha is sifted in, then 60°C water is added and whisked in a clean W until a soft, mossy foam crowns the surface.

~ 60 seconds

Rest & pour

The bowl rests a moment, the foam settles just so, and it is set in front of you with both hands. Drink it before it cools.

~ 3 minutes
