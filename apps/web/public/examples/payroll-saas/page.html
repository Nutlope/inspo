A pay run in four steps

Twenty minutes every other Wednesday. That is the whole job.

Most operators approve a pay run from their phone between lunch and dinner service. Backhouse does the collection, the math and the filing; you do the reviewing.

01

Shifts and tips sync overnight

Hours, breaks and card tips arrive from your point of sale and scheduling tools. Cash tips are declared by staff in the app.

02

Rules are applied and checked

Overtime, tip credits, split shifts and state wage floors are calculated. Anything that looks off gets a flag with a plain explanation.

03

You review and approve

One screen with every person, every hour and every dollar. Fix a shift inline, then approve. Managers can be limited to their own location.

04

Friday, everyone is paid

Direct deposit lands on pay day, taxes are withheld and filed, and staff see a pay stub that separates wages from tips.

Encrypted in transit and at rest

Every record, from a clock-in to a bank account number, is encrypted with keys rotated on a schedule. Staff never see one another's pay.

Independently audited every year

An outside firm reviews our controls annually and the report is available to customers under a standard agreement. The current report covers 2025.

Payroll funds are held apart from ours

Money you fund for a pay run sits in a segregated account at a federally insured partner bank until it is paid out. It is never used to run Backhouse.

Tax filing guarantee

If a filing we made is late or wrong, we pay the penalty and the interest. Since 2021 that has happened zero times, and we intend to keep it there.

Access limited by role and location

Owners see everything, managers see their own location, staff see their own stubs. Every change to a pay run is logged with who made it and when.
