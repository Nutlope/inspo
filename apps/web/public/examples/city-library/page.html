A library for the whole street, open until 9 four nights a week.

Larkspur Branch is Halden's first new library in twenty-two years: two floors of books, quiet rooms, a makerspace and a hall, on the corner where the old tram depot stood. Membership is free, and everyone is welcome.

Opening hours

Open late, because the day ends at six for most of us.

Most libraries close as people leave work. Larkspur keeps the lights on until nine, Monday to Thursday, with staff on every floor and the cafe serving until eight.

  • Return chute and holds lockers in the lobby work around the clock, every day of the year.
  • Study rooms can be booked up to 8:30 pm on late nights. The reading room goes silent after six.
  • Public holidays: 10 am to 5 pm. Closed on 25 and 26 December and 1 January.

Rooms and services

Two floors, a roof garden, and eight rooms with a job to do.

Designed by Orme and Keel Architects inside the shell of the 1911 tram depot. The sawtooth roof and the iron columns stayed; everything under them is new.

Membership

Membership is free. It always has been.

One card works at every Halden library, on the mobile library, and for ebooks, audiobooks and newspapers at home. There are no joining fees and, since 2022, no late fines at all.

Join at the desk on 3 October → Join online, collect in person

  1. Bring something with your name and address

    A letter, a bill, a bank card, a phone with your address on it. Anything reasonable does.

  2. Borrow up to twenty things for three weeks

    Renew online up to five times as long as nobody else is waiting. Return to any branch, or the chute.

  3. Under sixteen? Join with a grown-up

    A parent or carer signs once. Children's cards never carry charges, even for a lost picture book.

  4. No address yet? Still welcome

    A temporary card gives you two items and full computer access while you settle in.

Getting here

14 Larkspur Street, Halden 4021On the corner of Larkspur Street and Depot Lane, opposite Mill Park. The entrance is on Larkspur Street, with a second door from the park after dark.

  • Tram

    Line 2 and Line 5 to Halden Central, two minutes on foot along Depot Lane.

    Trams run until 12:40 am on late nights.
  • Bus

    Routes 14, 27 and 41 stop outside the front door at the Larkspur Street stop.

    The 14 runs every eight minutes until midnight.
  • Bike

    Forty stands on the forecourt, covered, lit and in view of the desk.

    A pump and a repair stand are fixed by the door.
  • Car

    Six blue badge bays on Depot Lane. Mill Park car park is 300 metres away, free after 6 pm.

    No general parking on Larkspur Street itself.
  • On foot

    Step-free from every entrance, with a lift to each floor and the roof garden.

    Changing places toilet on the ground floor.
