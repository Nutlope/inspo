The route

One loop, eight neighbourhoods, 214 m of climb.

Start on Harbour Square, climb gently through Old Town to Northgate, cross Kestrel Park, swing round the university and come home along the River Calder and the waterfront to Cathedral Green.

Course records

Fast enough to chase, flat enough to catch.

Three records have fallen in the last three editions. The course is certified for qualifying times, and the last 12 km are almost entirely at sea level with the wind behind you.

2:05:41

Men's course record

Tesfaye Alemu, 2024. Ran the second half in 62:08 and broke the mark by 51 seconds.

2:19:08

Women's course record

Hanna Lindqvist, 2025. A national record, set alone from 30 km.

1:28:52

Wheelchair course record

Marcus Okafor, 2025. Broke his own record from 2023 by over two minutes.

Course record bonuses in 2027: £25,000 for a men's or women's record, £8,000 for the wheelchair record, on top of a £180,000 prize purse split equally across the three divisions.

Spectator points and road closures

Where to stand, and where not to drive.

Five official cheer zones with music, toilets, coffee and a big screen. Each one is a short walk from a tram stop, and you can see the leaders and the last wave from the same spot within four hours.

1

Northgate, 10 kmleaders from 07:30

The first big crowd of the day. Runners come through twice within a minute on either side of the central reservation. Tram stop Northgate.

2

University Bridge, 25 kmleaders from 08:15

Elevated views down onto the field as it turns for home. Students run the loudest zone on the course. Tram stop University.

3

River Calder south bank, 30 kmleaders from 08:30

Where the race gets decided. Quiet enough to shout a name and be heard. Ten minutes on foot from Cathedral Green, so you can see two points.

4

Waterfront Drive, 35 kmleaders from 08:45

Full sun, sea air and 800 metres of dead straight road. Bring a sign. Tram stop Marina.

5

Cathedral Green, finishleaders from 09:05

Grandstand seats are £12, standing is free along the last 300 metres. Family reunion area by letter behind the medal tent.
