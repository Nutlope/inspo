About Subtone

We started in a basement in Neukölln with one pressing and a borrowed mixer. Twelve years and forty-one records later, the brief hasn't changed: music for low rooms and long nights.

Subtone is artist-owned and stubbornly independent. Every record is mastered for the room it'll be played in, cut to vinyl, and documented in a catalogue we keep like a logbook.

We press small, repress when it matters, and put the music first. Demos are open — send us something for the dark.
