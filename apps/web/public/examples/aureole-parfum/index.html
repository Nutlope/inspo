01 — The Composition

Three movements, eleven materials, nothing decorative.

Head · first 20 minutes

Cold and bright

A short opening on purpose. White pepper over Calabrian bergamot, which burns off quickly and is meant to.

  • White pepper, Madagascar
  • Bergamot, Calabria
  • Pink peppercorn

Heart · two to five hours

The whole point

Haitian vetiver, distilled twice and stripped of its smoke, held in suspension by orris butter from Tuscany.

  • Vetiver, Haiti · double-distilled
  • Orris butter, Tuscany
  • Angelica root
  • Violet leaf absolute

Base · six hours onward

What stays

Dry cedar and ambrette, which reads more like warm skin than like wood. No vanilla, no musk cocktail.

  • Virginian cedar
  • Ambrette seed
  • Labdanum, trace
  • Cashmeran
