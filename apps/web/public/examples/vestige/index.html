The exhibition

What is left after the pouring is done.

A vessel is not the thing it holds. It is the shape of the having-held.

Curated by gallery director Mira Ostend, Quiet Vessels brings together twelve artists working across ceramic, woven textile, 16 mm film and cast bronze. Each was asked a single question: what does the container become when you stop filling it?

The answers occupy three rooms. In the first, the vessel as body - fragile, glazed, subject to heat. In the second, the vessel as room - architecture, absence, the negative space of a doorway. In the third, the vessel as memory - what we carry forward when the object itself is gone.
