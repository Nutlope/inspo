First Features

Twenty-two debuts, all of them made for less than the catering budget of a studio picture. Every director is in the room for the Q&A.

Horror, transgression, and whatever arrived in the submission pile that we could not classify. Doors at 22:00, no admittance after the titles.

Restorations and orphan prints, projected on 16mm where a print survives. Six programmes, none of them streaming anywhere.
