03 Safety

The robot slows before it needs to stop.

Two 270 degree safety scanners cover every side of the chassis. Field sets are switched by speed and load, so the protective field is always longer than the braking distance, with margin.

  • ISO 3691-4:2023Driverless industrial trucks. Full type examination by TÜV SÜD, certificate on file for all three chassis.
  • ISO 13849-1Safety functions rated PL d, Category 3: scanner fields, speed monitoring, emergency stop, lift interlock.
  • IEC 61496-3Type 3 safety laser scanners, two per robot, 270 degree scan, 40 ms response.
  • UL 3100Listed for North American sites. CE marked under the Machinery Regulation 2023/1230 for the EU.
  • ANSI/ITSDF B56.5Compliant for US deployments including the audible and visual warning requirements.

04 Yard software

Yard runs the floor. Your WMS still runs the warehouse.

Yard is the fleet manager that ships with every robot. It takes transport orders from your WMS, assigns them, routes traffic, schedules charging and reports back. It runs on a single on-site server with a cloud mirror for support.

  • 4.1

    Native integrations

    SAP EWM, Manhattan Active, Blue Yonder and Körber connectors maintained by Halden. Anything else over REST or MQTT.

  • 4.2

    VDA 5050 2.1 compliant

    Third-party AGVs from other vendors can share the floor under Yard traffic control, or Halden robots can run under yours.

  • 4.3

    Traffic management for 400 robots per site

    Time-window reservation on a lane graph, with deadlock detection and priority lanes for outbound waves.

  • 4.4

    Charging that never blocks a wave

    Opportunity charging between orders. Yard keeps every robot above 30% state of charge during the peak plan you give it.

  • 4.5

    Simulation before commissioning

    Load your layout and last month's order file. Yard tells you the fleet size and the throughput before a single robot arrives.

05 Deployment

Eight weeks from survey to first shift, on a running warehouse.

No floor modification, no downtime. We map your building while it operates and commission robots one aisle at a time.

Week 0

Site survey

One engineer, one day. Floor flatness, aisle widths, dock geometry, Wi-Fi coverage and the order profile from your WMS.

Output: fleet size and throughput estimate

Weeks 1 to 2

Map and simulate

A single HX-1400 maps the building during a normal shift. Yard simulates the last 30 days of orders on the real map.

Output: signed layout and lane graph

Weeks 3 to 6

Pilot on one flow

Five robots on dock-to-rack only, mixed with your existing traffic. Safety fields are validated on site with your officer present.

Output: acceptance test report

Week 8

Go live and scale

Full fleet arrives pre-configured. Adding a robot later is a serial number in Yard and a 45 minute charge.

Output: production fleet, 24/7 support
