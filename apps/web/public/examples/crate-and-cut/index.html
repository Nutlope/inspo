Our ethos - filed under: why a record store still matters in 2025.

We believe a record store should smell like dust, sound like a needle drop, and treat every crate like it's got gold at the bottom.

Crate & Cut opened in 2014 in a 400-square-foot space off Bedford Ave. We're still there - just with more turntables, a listening station that gets daily use, and a staff of working DJs and collectors who'll tell you the truth about every piece of gear. No drop-ship, no algorithmic recommendations. We listen to it, we stock it, we stand behind it.
