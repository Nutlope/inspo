Six halls, rotated twice a year. Everything is at kid height, which is why the grown-ups end up kneeling.

Floor plan at the desk

Hall 1

The Storm Room

Make real lightning behind very thick glass, then find out why your hair has opinions about it.

Ages 6 and up

Hall 2

Water Works

Dams, locks, whirlpools and an Archimedes screw the size of a slide. Ponchos provided, dignity not.

All ages · You will get wet

Hall 3

Small Universe

A planetarium for short attention spans: eleven minutes, one comet, and the loudest sun we could legally build.

Shows on the hour

Hall 4

Take It Apart

Real appliances, real screwdrivers, zero instructions. The one room where breaking it is the assignment.

Ages 8 and up

Hall 5

The Ball Run

Forty metres of track, six gates, and a queue of adults pretending they are here to supervise.

All ages

Hall 6

Meet Rivet

Our robot. Rivet sorts blocks, loses at noughts and crosses on purpose, and waves back every single time.
