Honest answers

Things people ask.

FAQ · 05 / 05

Can't find what you're after? Write us — a human reads it, usually Sam, usually the same day. hello@slowboat.co

How fresh is "fresh"?+
Most subscription bags arrive within 4–7 days of roasting. We roast in small batches on Monday and Tuesday and ship same-day. Each bag has the roast date printed on the tin tie — not the "best by," the actual date.
Can I skip a week?+
Yes, from any email or in your account. Skip one shipment, skip three, pause until you say go again. Your schedule shouldn't be a contract.
What if I want to try before I commit?+
Order any single bag from the shop. If you subscribe within 30 days, we'll credit the cost of that bag against your first plan shipment. No code required — we'll see it on our end.
Whole bean or ground?+
Whole bean keeps freshness longest, but we'll grind for almost any method — V60, Aeropress, Chemex, Moka, French press, espresso. Tell us your gear and we'll match it.
Where do you ship?+
All 50 US states, free of charge on every subscription. Canada and the UK ship via our partner roasters at a small carbon-offset surcharge. Everywhere else, soon.
What happens if I hate it?+
Email us. We'll refund the bag and send a different roast for free, our pick. We'd rather you stay than save the cost of a bag of coffee.

A letter, once a month.

New origins, brewing essays, and the occasional discount that we mean. No re-engagement campaigns. We promise.
