The cave

A quarry tunnel that has not changed temperature since the 1850s.

Coldharbour was cut for Bath stone and abandoned in 1961. The four founding farms leased it in 1987 because it did the one thing a refrigerator cannot: it holds eleven degrees and ninety-two percent humidity through August heat and January frost, without a single fan. Every wheel we make spends its whole life on spruce boards down there, brushed, turned and tasted by two affineurs who walk the full length of the tunnel each morning.

  • MondayEvery wheel under six months turned and brushed. Boards wiped with brine.
  • WednesdayReserve wheels over a year checked with an iron; any that are ready are marked for the shop.
  • FridayPriddy Blue pierced or moved deeper into the wet end. New make brought down from the dairy.
  • SaturdayTours at 11:00 and 14:00. The tunnel is closed to visitors the rest of the week.

Seasonal notes

The cheese follows the grass, and the grass follows the year.

  • SpringMarch to May

    Now in season

    The goats kid in February and Wellow Ash returns to the shop in the second week of March, after a winter without it. Cows go out on the new grass from mid-April and the milk changes almost overnight: thinner, greener, higher in yield. The Hollow Wall made from it is the freshest tasting of the year.

    Best nowWellow Ash, Hollow Wall, the last of the winter Reserve

  • SummerJune to August

    Now in season

    Full pasture milk, deep yellow from the buttercups and clover. This is the milk that becomes next year's Reserve, so the make room runs at capacity and the tunnel fills. We stop courier deliveries beyond the van radius when the forecast passes 26 degrees. Ember is not made in summer.

    Best nowMendip Tomme, Wellow Ash, Priddy Blue picnics

  • AutumnSeptember to November

    Now in season

    The Reserve release. Wheels made the summer before last are ironed in September and the best come up to the shop from the first Saturday in October. Smoking starts when the nights turn cold. The goats dry off in November and the Ash disappears until March; buy it while it lasts.

    Best nowColdharbour Reserve, Ember, Priddy Blue

  • WinterDecember to February

    Now in season

    Hay milk: cows are in, feeding on meadow hay, and the milk is richer and slower to set. Whole Reserve wheels and half Tommes for Christmas must be ordered by the first Sunday in December; the van runs an extra day in the week before Christmas. The shop shuts for four days over the holiday.

    Best nowWhole wheels, Ember, the Blue at its peak
Visiting

The shop is the old milking parlour, next to the make room.

You can watch the morning make through the window until about eleven, taste everything on the counter, and take a cave tour on Saturdays. There is a small café doing toasties, soup and our own whey-fed pork sausage rolls. Dogs on leads are welcome in the yard, not in the shop or the cave.

Coldharbour Dairy Co-operative, Coldharbour Lane, Priddy, near Wells, Somerset. Follow the brown signs from the Priddy green; the lane is single track and the car park is on the left before the dairy.
