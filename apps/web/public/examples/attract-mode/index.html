Restoration, in the order it actually happens.

A cabinet takes eighty to two hundred volunteer hours. The wood is the easy part.

Step 1

Stabilise, don't strip

Original side art stays, scratches and all. We seal it, we never repaint over it; a 1981 scar is part of the machine's memory.

Step 2

The board gets a bath

Every capacitor tested, every socket reflowed, ROMs dumped and archived before we touch anything. The dump goes to three offsite copies first.

Step 3

The tube keeps its glow

We recap and rejuvenate original CRTs rather than swap in flat panels. When a tube finally dies, the cabinet waits for a donor; it does not get a television.
