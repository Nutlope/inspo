04 / FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

What counts as a billable seat?

Only active members. Guests on Team and Enterprise are free within their allotment, and deactivated members stop billing at the next cycle. We true-up monthly, so you never pay for empty seats.

Can we switch plans or cancel mid-cycle?

Yes. Upgrades apply immediately and are prorated; downgrades and cancellations take effect at the end of the current cycle. Your vault data stays exportable in plaintext or encrypted formats at any time.

What happens to our data if we leave?

You can export everything before you go. After cancellation the vault becomes read-only for 90 days, then ciphertext is permanently deleted. We hold no keys, so there is nothing for us to read in the meantime.

Do you offer a free plan?

Every tier has a 14-day full-featured trial with no card. For individuals we keep a free personal vault (1 user, unlimited items) that upgrades cleanly into a team later.

How does self-hosting work on Enterprise?

You run the same server we do, shipped as a signed container with an offline license. Updates are pull-based on your schedule, and our team supports your deployment under the same SLA as cloud.
