06Core botanicals, traced from soil to skin

Six plants. One philosophy.

Every formula begins on foot - in the damp understory of the Olympic foothills, where we harvest resins, roots and leaves by hand at the moment they're most active. We never take more than a tenth of a stand, and we return every spent batch to the soil it came from.

Nothing is bleached, deodorised or stretched with fillers. What you smell is the forest - cedar smoke, wet bark, the faint sweetness of sap - and what you feel is the work of plants that have been tending skin for far longer than we have.
