Security model

Encryption happens on your device. Not ours.

Your master password never leaves your machine. We store only ciphertext - a scrambled blob even we can't read. Here's the chain of trust.

Zero-knowledge by design Your master password is hashed locally with Argon2id. We receive an encryption key we mathematically cannot reverse.
XChaCha20-Poly1305 Every credential is encrypted with authenticated symmetric cryptography before it ever touches sync. Tampering is detected, not just prevented.
Open source, audited The crypto library is MIT-licensed and peer-reviewed. Independent audits by Cure53 (2024) and Trail of Bits (2025) found zero critical issues.

Features

Everything a vault should do. Nothing it shouldn't.

Password generator that actually resists cracking

Generate passphrases and random strings with full entropy control. Every character drawn from a CSPRNG - never Math.random. Tunable length, character classes, and Diceware word lists for human-typable strength.

$ vaultline generate --length 24 --diceware
→ correct-horse-battery-staple-9!Qz
entropy: 156 bits · crack time: 4.8×10³⁶ years
CSPRNG · Diceware · Entropy meter

Unified search

Find any credential by name, URL, or tag in <30ms. Spotlight-style, keyboard-first, available everywhere.

⌘K · fuzzy · offline

Breach monitor

Continuously checks12B+ leaked credentials. Alerts you the moment a stored login appears in a breach corpus.

k-anonymity · HIBP API

Autofill that respects your privacy

Browser extensions and native apps fill credentials directly from the encrypted vault - no clipboard, no intermediate plaintext. Fields are matched on-device against stored entry metadata, so autofill never leaks which sites you use to any third party.

Native autofill · no clipboard · on-device matching

Secure sharing

Share a credential with end-to-end encryption. Recipients decrypt with a passphrase you share out-of-band.

E2E · expiring links
