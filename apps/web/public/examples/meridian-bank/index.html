/ 06 — questions

The fine print, in plain English.

Still wondering something? Write to hello@meridian.bank — a human reads it.

Is Meridian a bank?
Meridian is a financial technology company, not a bank. Banking services are provided by our partner banks, Members FDIC. Deposits are held at those institutions and swept across a network of program banks for up to $5M of FDIC insurance — 20× the standard $250k per-bank limit.
How does treasury stay liquid if it earns yield?
Treasury balances are invested in government money-market funds, which settle next business day. You set a floor to keep in checking for daily operations; everything above it earns the current yield. There’s no lockup, no notice period, and no penalty for pulling funds back.
What does it actually cost?
Standard is free — checking, treasury, cards, ACH, and domestic wires at no charge, with no minimum balance. Scale is $50/mo and adds bill pay, approval workflows, roles, and accounting sync. We never charge overdraft, maintenance, or hidden statement fees; FX is passed through at the mid-market rate.
Can I switch from my current bank without downtime?
Yes. Open the account in about nine minutes, then run both in parallel while you migrate. We’ll help move recurring payments, payroll, and vendor details, and your old account stays live until you’ve fully cut over. Most teams finish the switch in under two weeks.
Who is Meridian built for?
Incorporated US businesses — from a two-founder startup to a finance team closing the books for a few hundred employees. If you reconcile a general ledger, run approvals, or hold meaningful cash, Meridian was designed for the way you already work. We don’t serve personal accounts.
Does my accounting tool plug in?
QuickBooks Online, Xero, and NetSuite sync natively on Scale and above — transactions arrive coded, matched, and ready to approve. Everything else is exportable to CSV, OFX, or our REST API, so your data is never locked inside a dashboard.
