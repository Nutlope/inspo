Where the numbers come from

Three models, one hundred and twenty seven sensors, and no averaging that hides a gust.

When the models disagree we show you the disagreement. The board carries the spread, not just the mean, so you can see when a forecast is confident and when it is guessing.

6min

Refresh cadence for wind, pressure and wave once a new run is ingested.

0.1deg

Ocean grid from ECMWF, sharpened to 0.025° within 30 nm of the coast.

127

Buoys, lightships and coastal stations feeding the nowcast layer.

14days

Forecast horizon, with spread shown from day four onwards.

Global GFS 0.25° and ECMWF IFS 0.1°

Four runs a day. Pressure fields drive the low and ridge tracking on the board; wind is bias-corrected against the buoy network every cycle.

Coastal Arome 0.025° and Harmonie

High-resolution layers for the Channel, Biscay, the Irish Sea and the Baltic. These catch the land breeze and headland acceleration the globals smooth away.

Observed Buoys, satellite scatterometer, AIS weather reports

Every reading within 60 nm of your waypoint is plotted with its age. A number older than 90 minutes is greyed, never quietly dropped.

Beyond phone signal

The whole board in an 18 kB email, built for Iridium.

Send a one-line request from any satellite mailer and Halyard replies with a compressed packet: a 48 hour wind and wave grid around your position, the tide table for your destination, and the area forecast as text. The app renders it offline.

  • Works with Iridium Go, Starlink and Inmarsat mailers. Plain text in, plain text out, no attachment blocking.
  • Choose your resolution. 0.5° for a quick look at 6 kB, 0.1° for a routing decision at 18 kB.
  • Positions from your log. Reply to a packet and the next one is centred on where you are now.
