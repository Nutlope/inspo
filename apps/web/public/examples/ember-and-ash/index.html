02 / How It's Made

Ninety days is the recipe.

01

Grown, not sourced

Every chili comes from four farms inside a ninety-mile radius. We know the rows.

02

Smoked over almond

Six hours of cool smoke. Almond wood burns sweeter than hickory and never turns acrid.

03

Wild ferment

Salt, mash, patience. No starter culture. Ninety days in a barrel that used to hold zinfandel.

04

Bottled by hand

Four hundred at a time, labelled and dated in the room where it was made.
