Lead Essay · p. 014

On the dignity of the second life

There is a bowl on my shelf that has been broken twice and is more beautiful for it. The seams are gold — kintsugi, the Japanese art of mending with lacquer and powdered metal — and they trace the bowl's history the way a scar traces a life. We were taught to hide damage. The Repair Issue is an argument for the opposite: that a thing worth keeping is a thing worth fixing, in the open, where the fixing can be seen.

Marguerite Okonkwo, Editor
