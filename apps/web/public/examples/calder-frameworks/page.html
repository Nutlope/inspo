Twenty-two weeks, five stages, one pair of hands.

One builder does every frame start to finish. That is why the book is short and the lead time is honest.

1

Week 0 · two hours

A full-size drawing and a chart like the one above. Nothing is cut until you have signed the sheet.

Assembled as a frameset or a full bike, with the geometry chart, a lug rubbing and the drawing rolled in a tube.

Construction

Why lugs, why silver, why steel.

Lugs carry the joint

A lug is a cast steel sleeve that holds two tubes at a fixed angle. The braze fills the gap between sleeve and tube, so the joint is a long overlap in shear, not a bead of weld in tension. It is the reason a lugged frame from the seventies still rides.

Silver, not brass, at the tubes

We braze the main triangle with 56 percent silver at around 650 degrees, well below the point where heat-treated steel loses its temper. Brass is reserved for the dropouts and braze-ons, where its strength matters more than the heat.

Steel you can repair

A bent dropout is straightened. A dented tube is replaced by unbrazing the lug and sliding a new one in. We have re-tubed frames we built a decade ago and frames other people built forty years ago.

Filed by hand, then thinned

Every lug edge is filed to a knife line and the shoreline of braze taken back until the tube seems to grow out of the lug. Under paint it shows; under polish it is the whole point.

Questions we get in the workshop

Asked at the bench, answered here.

I have never been fitted. Where do I start?

Bring the bike you ride most, even if you dislike it. We measure you and the bike, ride the jig for an hour, and the chart falls out of that. Most people leave knowing why their current bike hurts.

Can you build for disc brakes and thru-axles?

Yes. The Holme is flat-mount disc with 12 mm thru-axles front and rear. The Ridgeway can be built for disc, though the lugged fork becomes a segmented crown fork to take the load.

Do you repair frames you did not build?

Steel ones, yes: dents, bent dropouts, cracked stays, replacing a tube. We do not touch aluminium or carbon. Send photos first; most repairs are quoted within a day and take two to three weeks.

What about a frame for a child, or a tandem?

Children's frames are a joy and we build one or two a year at a reduced price. Tandems we refer to a friend in Yorkshire who has the jig for it.

What does the guarantee actually cover?

The brazed joints, for the life of the original owner. Paint is guaranteed for two years against lifting. Crashes and rust from a winter in the garden are not covered, but both are repairable.
