Three things happen. You do none of them.

Even sits behind your invoicing, not in front of it. You keep sending invoices the way you already do; the only difference is what your balance says afterwards.

01

An invoice clears

Connect the tool you already invoice with, or forward the receipt. Even watches for money actually landing, not for invoices being sent, because those are different days and you know it.

02

The slice moves out

Your rate is applied to that payment alone and the amount is transferred to a separate, interest-bearing account in your name. It happens same-day, before the money starts looking spendable.

03

The bill is already paid for

When the quarter or the year ends, the number you owe is sitting in an account you have not touched. Even tells you the date, the figure, and where to send it. Nothing to scramble for.

Built for income that arrives in lumps

Most savings tools assume a salary on the 25th. Freelance money comes in a shape, not a rhythm, and every feature here follows from that.

A balance that tells the truth

The number most freelancers panic about is the one in their current account, which is always wrong, because it contains someone else's tax. Even splits the two permanently, so the figure you look at is the figure you can actually spend.

The questions people actually ask

Mostly asked by designers, writers and contractors who have been burned once and would like not to be again.

01 What percentage should I use?

Even cannot tell you: it depends on your country, your allowances, your deductible costs and whether you also have salaried income. Most people start from what their last return worked out to, then adjust once they have a better figure. If you have an accountant, ask them for the number and put that in.

02 What if I set aside too much?

Then you have savings. The money in the tax account is yours: withdraw it, lower the rate, or leave it to cover the next quarter. Even does not lock, freeze or forward anything to a tax authority.

03 Does it file my taxes?

No. Even is a savings account with an automation attached. It does not prepare returns, calculate liability, or give tax advice. It makes sure the money is there when someone who does those things tells you the figure.

04 What happens when a client pays late, or not at all?

Nothing moves. Even acts on cleared payments only, so an unpaid invoice never causes a set-aside. If a payment is later refunded or reversed, the matching set-aside is reversed with it.

05 Which invoicing tools does it connect to?

Even reads cleared payments from your connected account, so it works regardless of what you invoice with. Direct connections to specific invoicing tools are listed during setup, and forwarding a payment receipt works as a fallback.

06 What does it cost?

No monthly fee for the account. Full pricing, including anything charged on transfers, is shown before you open an account. Nothing is buried in a signup flow.
