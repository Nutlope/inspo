01  The shape of a freelance year

Twelve months of getting paid,
none of them the same size.

A salaried person's tax comes off before they ever see it. Yours arrives whole, feels like yours, and then leaves in one lump several months later. Even puts the split back where it belongs: at the moment of payment.

02  How it works

Three things, then it's quiet.

1

Connect the account you invoice into

Even reads incoming payments from the account your clients already pay. Read-only where it can be: nothing leaves that account except into your own reserve.

2

Set the share you want held back

One number, chosen by you or by your accountant. If you have no idea, start high: an over-full reserve is a much cheaper mistake than an empty one. Change it whenever your year changes.

3

Every payment splits itself

An invoice clears, and its share moves to your reserve the same day. Your spendable balance is the honest one, so a good month stops looking like more money than it is.

04  Built for the awkward parts

Freelance money is strange.
The tool should know that.

The client who pays in ninety days

Nothing is reserved on the promise of an invoice. Money only moves when money actually arrives, so a late payer never drains your spendable balance.

The one enormous month

A project that lands all at once is where over-spending happens. Even takes its share off the top before the number has a chance to feel like a windfall.

Deadlines that aren't monthly

Quarterly, annual, whatever your jurisdiction insists on. Mark the dates you owe against and the reserve is measured against them rather than against a calendar month.

More than one thing to put aside

Income tax is rarely the only claim on a payment. Keep separate reserves with their own rates and read them as one spendable number.

05  Questions people ask first

The awkward ones, answered plainly.

What if I pick the wrong percentage?
You change it, and the new rate applies to payments from that moment on. Earlier payments keep the rate they were reserved at, so your history stays true to what actually happened. Even shows you the blended rate across the year so you can see whether you're drifting.
Can I take money back out of the reserve?
Yes. It's your money sitting in your reserve, not an escrow you are locked out of. Releasing it takes a deliberate action and shows up in your activity so that a quiet raid on the tax pot is never something you do by accident.
Does Even file anything for me?
No. Even sets money aside and keeps a clean record of what was reserved against which payment. Filing stays with you or your accountant, who can be given read access to the same ledger.
What happens in a month where nothing lands?
Nothing. There is no monthly sweep and no fixed amount taken on a date. A month with no income is a month with no reserving, which is the whole reason a percentage of payments works better here than a standing order.
Do I have to move my banking?
No. Keep invoicing into the account you already use. Even connects to it, watches for incoming payments, and holds your reserve separately.
