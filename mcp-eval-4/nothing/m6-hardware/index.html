02 · the screen

A screen that behaves like paper.

E-ink reflects the room's light instead of shining its own at you. Noon on a park bench is its best light, and a dim train still reads like a paperback. When you pause to think, the page simply stays there, holding your place and sipping nothing.

03 · the keyboard

Keys that push back.

Typing on glass is typing on a photograph of a keyboard. Vellum's keys travel, land, and answer, so your hands can carry the sentence while your head writes the next one. It is the same QWERTY you already speak; muscle memory moves in on day one.

04 · the battery

Gone all week.

One charge covers a week of writing. Vellum leaves the house on Monday and comes home with pages, not a low-battery warning. No charger in the bag, no outlet anxiety at the cafe: the routine is Sunday night, beside the house keys.
