Common questions · the back-of-the-issue

Things people actually ask.

Is my money safe?

Yes. Cash sits at our partner bank (Cross River, member FDIC), insured to $250,000. Investments are held at Apex Clearing, SIPC-protected to $500,000. Compound itself never custodies your funds. Read the security note →

What's the catch?

Standard is genuinely free. We make money from the spread on the cash sleeve and a small fee on the Compound 100. The Studio and Together tiers exist for power features. No ads, no selling your data, no nudges to "buy more crypto."

Can I pick my own stocks?

On purpose, no. Picking-your-own is a great way to underperform the index by 3% a year on average. Compound is the stop-picking-stocks app — that's the entire pitch.

How fast can I get my money out?

Cash is same-day. Invested funds settle T+1 (one business day). No early-withdrawal fees, ever — it's your money.

Is this a robo-advisor?

Sort of. We're a registered investment adviser with the SEC (CRD #309442), but we built one fund instead of fifty. You don't pick a "risk profile" out of a quiz; you hold the market and let it compound.
