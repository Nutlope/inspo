The practice

Three small habits, repeated without ceremony.

We borrowed from monastic rule and modern CBT in equal parts. None of it is mystical. None of it requires belief.

  1. Step 01 — Morning

    A short sit, before the inbox.

    Five to ten minutes of guided attention. Different teacher each day, so it never settles into a script you stop hearing.

  2. Step 02 — Anywhere

    One breath, when you remember.

    A pocket library of 90-second resets — for meetings, traffic, the moment between texts. Three taps, in, out.

  3. Step 03 — At night

    A story you don't finish.

    Sleep stories read low and slow by writers we admire. Most listeners are out by minute four. The story continues without you.

The library

Built like a small private radio station.

Every session is original. Every voice is credited. We file them by mood, hour, and duration — the way you'd file records on a shelf.

  • Section A — Sound

    Three hundred original sessions, read aloud.

    Stilla's library is recorded in a small studio in Lisbon. No royalty-free voiceover, no AI narration — just teachers reading their own words. Listen to the same teacher all month, or pull a stranger's voice at random.

Six common ones
I've tried meditation. It didn't work.

Most apps treat meditation like a habit that "sticks" — like flossing. We treat it like reading: you don't read because it's good for you; you read because you like a particular author. Stilla is built around voices and short forms so you find a teacher you actually want to spend ten minutes with.

Is the 14-day trial really no-card?

Yes. You give us an email; we give you a link. If you've still got the app on day 15, we'll ask for a card then. If you've deleted it, we never hear from each other again.

What's actually in a "session"?

A short introduction (sometimes thirty seconds, never three minutes), a guided sit, and a single closing thought you can take into the day. Lengths run from five to twenty minutes; default is nine.

Who are the teachers?

Seventeen of them, currently. Three Theravāda monks, two psychotherapists, a sleep researcher, two published poets, and nine independent meditation teachers we found by ear. Every teacher is credited on every track they read.

Is this private?

Sessions stream encrypted. We don't sell data; we don't have ads; we don't run analytics on what you listened to last night. Account deletion takes one tap and one confirmation.

What happens after the trial?

£3.20 a month, billed once a year. That's the price; it has been since we started, and we don't plan to change it. Two seats per account so you can share it with someone at home.
