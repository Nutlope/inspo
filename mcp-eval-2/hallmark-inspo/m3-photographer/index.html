About the studio

Filed by hand

I started photographing in 2017 because a friend asked me to make a picture of her grandmother, and the picture came out fine. The next one was better. I have been chasing that small difference ever since.

The studio is one room in an apartment near Castelo de São Jorge, with a north-facing window and a borrowed Hasselblad. Most of my work begins there: a kettle, a long conversation, eventually a camera. I prefer to sit with people before I photograph them, and to come back twice if the first sitting did not feel like enough.

Travel work happens between commissions, in seven- or ten-day stretches. I keep a small notebook for each trip and try not to publish anything from it for at least six months. The point is to let the pictures cool down before deciding which ones are worth keeping.

I take on three or four commissions a year. If the work below looks like something you would want, write.
