Dear restless reader,

I’m Maya. I sit for ten minutes every morning — sometimes well, often badly — and I’ve made Stillwater for the mornings where badly is the best you can do.

It isn’t an app you open and scroll. It’s a short letter, sent before dawn, with a single guided sitting at the bottom — seven minutes on a weekday, twelve on a Sunday. No streaks. No badges. No leaderboard. If you skip a day, the next morning’s letter still arrives, as if nothing happened, because nothing did.

The first fourteen days are free, and you can leave by replying with the word stop. We will not chase you. There’s no app to delete — only an email to ignore. That is, I think, the point.

Yours, in slowness,

Maya Okonkwo Co-founder · Stillwater

P.S. — if you’d like the first letter tomorrow at dawn, leave your address below. Fourteen mornings, on the house. No card, no commitment, no app to install.

A confirmation arrives within the hour. Unsubscribe with one word.

Until tomorrow, — The Stillwater desk
