Honest questions, plainly answered.

Is my money actually safe? +
Your cash sits in an FDIC-partnered sweep account up to $250,000; your invested balances are protected by SIPC up to $500,000 via our brokerage partner, Apex Clearing. None of that protects you from market losses — nothing does — but it does protect you from us going out of business.
What's the catch? Apps like this are usually free for a reason. +
Two reasons we're free under $1,000: it's a low-cost way to acquire long-term customers, and a $3,000 average balance is genuinely cheap to service at scale. Above $1,000 we charge a flat $3/month. We don't sell your spending data, and we don't run payment-for-order-flow on your trades. Read the disclosure.
Can I take it all out next week? +
Yes. No lock-up, no early-exit fee. You request a withdrawal, we sell the positions at next market close, the proceeds land in your linked bank in two business days. Average end-to-end: 3.5 days.
What if I know nothing about investing? +
That's the audience. The five named mixes are designed so you don't have to pick individual stocks. The weekly newsletter explains one piece of finance vocabulary per issue, in plain English — "expense ratio", "dollar-cost averaging", "rebalancing" — without making you feel small for asking.
