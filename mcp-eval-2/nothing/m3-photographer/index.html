01.
About — A note
from the photographer

I make photographs the way I take notes — slowly, by hand, mostly on film. My favorite frame is the one taken five seconds after a person stops posing.

I split my year between Lisbon and Lagos, and most of the rest on trains, buses, and the kind of small ferries that have to be flagged down. Whether I’m shooting a chef for a magazine cover or a riad for a hotel group, I’m looking for the same thing — weather, character, the texture of a place that doesn’t photograph itself.

I work small: usually it’s me, sometimes me and a single assistant, a Mamiya 7, and a stack of Portra. I edit my own scans. I keep things calm on set.

Mira Okafor
Photographer

03. Services — How we can work together

Three ways to commission.

All commissions are quoted bespoke; the rates below are starting points to help you plan a budget.
A · Portraits

Sittings & profile portraits.

Editorial or private. One subject, two hours, one location of your choosing — usually somewhere they’re comfortable rather than a studio.

  • Pre-shoot location scout
  • Up to 4 rolls of medium format
  • 20 edited high-res scans
  • Print-ready files within 14 days
From €1,400
B · Travel & Editorial

Stories on location.

Multi-day assignments for magazines, books, and tourism boards. I travel light and self-contained, and I’m happy to write the captions if you need them.

  • Day rate or project rate
  • Treatment + shotlist before travel
  • Color & B&W deliverables
  • Worldwide, visa & vaccinations sorted
From €2,200 / day
C · Hospitality & Brand

For hotels, restaurants, makers.

Imagery for hospitality groups, independent restaurants, and small product brands. Interiors, food, talent and people-in-place — all in one consistent language.

  • Half- and full-day rates
  • Lifestyle + still life in one shoot
  • Usage included for 24 months
  • Retouching by my long-time partner studio
From €3,600 / project
