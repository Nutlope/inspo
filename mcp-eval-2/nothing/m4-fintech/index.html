Questions

The things everyone asks before signing up.

Still nervous? Email hi@sprout.money — a human (not a bot) will reply within four hours.

01 Is my money actually safe?
Yes. Cash sits in FDIC-insured partner banks up to $250,000. Investments are protected by SIPC up to $500,000. We use 256-bit AES encryption, hardware-backed Face ID, and we never (ever) store your bank password.
02 What's the minimum to start investing?
One dollar. We use fractional shares, so a $0.78 round-up can actually buy you a sliver of a global index fund. No minimums, no waiting until you "have enough."
03 How do you make money?
Subscriptions on the Sprout and Canopy plans, plus interchange when you use the Sprout debit card. We don't sell your data, we don't take payment-for-order-flow, and we don't earn more when you trade more.
04 Can I withdraw whenever I want?
Always. Cash arrives in your linked bank within minutes. Invested funds settle in 1-2 business days like any brokerage. No lock-ins, no penalties.
05 What countries do you work in?
US, UK, EU (12 countries), Canada, and Australia. We're slowly opening more — drop your email below and we'll tell you the second your country goes live.
06 I have $0. Is this still for me?
Especially for you. Sprout was built for people starting from zero — the round-ups make it almost impossible to not save something. The first dollar is the hardest. We can help with that.
