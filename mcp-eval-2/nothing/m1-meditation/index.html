Slow answers

Questions, quietly answered.

If something here doesn’t answer yours, write to hello@stillwater.co. A real person replies, usually within a day.

Will I be billed during the trial?
No. We don’t take a card to start your trial. On day fourteen, if you’d like to keep listening, you can add a card — nothing happens until you do.
I’ve never meditated before. Is this for me?
Especially for you. Start with the First Week program — seven minutes a day, no posture rules, no mantra to memorise. By day three most people stop noticing they’re meditating at all.
Do you use AI to write or generate sessions?
Every script is written by a clinician and reviewed by a second one. Every voice you hear is a real human, paid above SAG-AFTRA rates, recorded in a real room. We won’t change that.
Can Stillwater replace therapy?
No, and we won’t pretend otherwise. Stillwater is a daily practice; therapy is a relationship. If you’re struggling, please reach out to a therapist — our Find Care page lists low-cost options in 14 countries.
What happens to my data?
Almost nothing. Listening history is stored locally on your device. Journal entries are end-to-end encrypted with a key only you hold. We don’t sell, and we don’t share — not even anonymised.
Where can I listen?
iOS, Android, the web, CarPlay, Apple Watch, and any browser. Sessions sync gently — no devices to authorise, no QR codes to scan.
